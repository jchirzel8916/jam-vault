Privacy Policy for JAM Vault

Last updated: November 2026

JAM Vault ("we", "our", or "app") is a personal collector tracking tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect & How It Is Used

JAM Vault connects to your Google account via OAuth solely to allow you to back up and restore your personal die-cast collection data directly to your own Google Drive storage.

  • Google Drive Data: The app only requests access to create and manage the specific application file (jam_vault_backup.json). We cannot access, view, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your name, email address, collection records, or photos on external servers. All data remains stored locally on your device and inside your personal Google Drive account.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data with any third parties.
