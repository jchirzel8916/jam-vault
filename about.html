🏁 About JAM Vault

JAM Vault is a dedicated garage management platform and collector companion built specifically for monster truck and die-cast enthusiasts. Designed for serious cataloging, family collections, and fleet tracking.

🚀 What is JAM Vault?

Whether you are managing a few favorite trucks or hundreds of models across multiple scales (1:64, Minis, 1:24), JAM Vault gives you full control over your fleet. With built-in factory specifications, custom color palettes, and photo sampling tools, you can accurately track every detail of your collection down to the chassis, rim, and tire compounds.

📖 How to Use the App

🏢 1. Organize Multiple Garages

Create distinct garages to separate collections by owner (e.g., Liam's Rigs, Carter's Vault), scale, or show display. Switch between garages or rename them with a single click.

📸 2. Add & Spec Rigs

Tap + Add Truck to pick from the 50-truck master roster (Grave Digger, Megalodon, Max-D, and more) or add custom models. Upload or snap photos directly on your phone.

🎨 3. Factory Color Matching

Tap a color component button (Body, Chassis, Rim, or Tire) and click on your truck photo. The built-in color engine identifies the closest authentic factory production finish.

🔍 4. Instant Filter & Search

Quickly locate trucks across your active garage by name, series, scale, chassis tint, or rim variant using the live search bar and filter controls.

☁️ Google Drive Cloud Backup

JAM Vault runs with a local-first architecture, meaning your garage data is always saved instantly on your current device—even without an internet connection.

💾

How to Sync Across Devices

1. Sign In: Tap the user pill in the top-right header and click Sign In with Google.
2. Backup: Select Backup to Google Drive to write an encrypted snapshot of all your garages directly into your private Google Drive storage.
3. Restore: Opening JAM Vault on a new phone, tablet, or PC? Simply sign in and click Restore from Google Drive to download your fleet in seconds.
